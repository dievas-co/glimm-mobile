## 📖 Introduction

**Glimm** is a **minimal thread-style diary app**

For modern individuals weary of complex social media and performative journaling, Glimm removes likes, comments, and notifications entirely—creating a digital sanctuary where you can focus solely on **your thoughts** and **the act of recording** itself.

## ✨ Key Features

* **🧵 Thread-Style Timeline**: Visualize daily thoughts as a seamless thread, connecting the flow of your ideas throughout the day.
* **🧘 Digital Zen**: A restrained **Black & White** interface (ink on paper) that eliminates visual clutter.
* **⏰ Spontaneous Journaling**: Random photo time notifications encourage you to capture moments in brief sessions rather than dedicating large blocks of time—building your day piece by piece.
